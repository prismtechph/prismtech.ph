/root
  ├── index.html          ← front page
  ├── home.html           ← main site container
  ├── /pages
  │     ├── navbar.html
  │     ├── about.html
  │     ├── services.html
  │     └── contact.html
  ├── /css
  │     └── style-home.css
  ├── /javascipt
  │     ├── matrix.js
  │     └── fetch.js
  ├── /image
      └── prismtechph-icon.png


/css → styles (style-home.css, style-index.css)

/image → all your logos/icons

/javascript → multiple scripts (fetch, home, index, matrix)

/pages → modular HTML snippets (navbar.html, maintenance.html, thankyou.html)

/php → send mail

==================================================================================================
Workflow
index.html → splash screen with Matrix + logo.
Clicking logo → redirects to home.html.
home.html → automatically fetches navbar.html and footer.html.
Navbar links → call loadSection() to fetch About, Services, Contact into <main id="content">.
